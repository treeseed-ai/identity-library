outcome: "TreeSeed Identity supplies shared authentication without granting application authorization or coupling sovereign installations to TreeSeed central."

---

TreeSeed Identity provides sovereign human and workload authentication through reusable OIDC, federation, session, and credential-exchange contracts.

Authentication must never imply team membership or application authority. Private installations remain independently operable, and federation remains explicit, directional, and non-transitive.

TreeDX is the sole authoring and publication authority for this objective.
